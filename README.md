## 🇨🇳 中文

从一张商品图片或商品描述加一个已确认卖点出发，选择能放大冲突的场景，创作一条由卖点直接推动剧情反转的广告，交付剧情创意、确认剧本、执行导演简报和成片。第一步只识别可见的商品类别、结构、数量、控制部件、附件、容器关系与安全操作方式，不从外观推断性能、容量、功率、安全或健康参数。确认商品与卖点后提供 7—8 个简短场景选项，每个都要说明一条具体冲突、压力、尺度对比或社会规则，以及它为何能让该卖点制造清楚翻转。创意默认开场不展示商品，先建立问题，再让商品第一次清楚入场成为转折的一部分：商品出现 → 启动或被使用 → 局面直接改变。硬检查要求拿掉商品和卖点反转就消失，对白只能点明画面已经证明的结果，不能替没有发生的画面兜底。不用于口播证言或与卖点无关的剧情短片。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 只按可见信息识别商品，不从外观推断性能、容量、功率或安全参数
- 按「商品＋卖点」生成 7—8 个带具体冲突、压力或尺度对比的场景选项
- 默认开场不展示商品，第一次清楚入场即参与转折而非装饰
- 同一次卖点事件内建立大而具体的前后倒置：进攻者变防守者、负担变富余等
- 创意硬检查：卖点因果、商品入场、直接倒置、场景贡献、合并结果、动机可读
- 交付剧情创意 + 确认剧本 + 执行导演简报（含人物动机桥梁与物理状态表）

## 🇬🇧 English

Starts from a product image or description plus one confirmed selling point, picks a scene that amplifies conflict, and builds an ad where that selling point directly drives a visible plot twist, delivering the creative concept, a confirmed script, an execution director's brief and the finished film. Step one identifies only what is visible — product category, structure, count, controls, accessories, container relationships and safe operation — and never infers performance, capacity, power, safety or health claims from looks. Once product and selling point are fixed, it offers 7–8 short scene options, each naming a concrete conflict, pressure, scale contrast or social rule and explaining why it makes the selling point produce a clear reversal. By default the product is absent from the opening: the problem is established first, then the product enters clearly as part of the turn — product appears, product starts or is used, the situation changes. Hard checks require the reversal to vanish if the product and selling point are removed, and dialogue may only name a result the picture already proved. Not for talking-head testimonials or plot shorts unrelated to the selling point.

### ✨ What it does

- Identifies the product only from visible facts, never inferring performance, capacity, power or safety claims from appearance
- Generates 7–8 scene options from the product-plus-selling-point pair, each with a concrete conflict, pressure or scale contrast
- Keeps the product out of the opening by default, so its first clear entrance is part of the turn rather than decoration
- Builds one large, concrete inversion inside a single selling-point event: attacker becomes defender, burden becomes surplus, and similar
- Hard creative checks: selling-point causality, product entrance, direct inversion, scene contribution, merged result, readable motivation
- Delivers the creative concept, a confirmed script and an execution director's brief with character-motivation bridges and physical-state tables
